Calculated from the Southend-on-Sea Ward Profiles (Directory 61) relative to the city-wide mean:

R_w = 0.25*(Age65+/Avg65+) + 0.20*(OwnerOcc/AvgOwner) + 0.25*(NonGrad/AvgNonGrad) + 0.30*(RightPool_2026/AvgRightPool)

2. Contest All 17 Wards vs. Reform UK Pact Mode

  • Contest All 17 Wards: Restore Britain stands in every ward (`V_RB = B * R_w * I_w`), absorbing the 2026 Confelicity/Heritage protest pool first and drawing the remainder from Reform UK, Conservatives, Independents, and Labour using your slider weights.
