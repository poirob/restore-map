StratMap Calibrated Econometric Methodology

1. Ward Demographic Receptivity Multiplier ($R_w$)

Benchmarked against Southend-on-Sea averages from official ONS Census and Southend Ward Profiles (Directory 61):

R_w = 0.25*(Age65+ / Avg65+) + 0.20*(OwnerOcc / AvgOwner) + 0.25*(NonGrad / AvgNonGrad) + 0.30*(RightPool / AvgRightPool)

2. Projected Restore Britain Vote Share ($V_{\text{RB}, w}$)

Scales the city-wide macro polling baseline ($B$) by ward receptivity ($R_w$) and campaign intensity ($I_w$), capped at 65%:

V_RB = min(65, B * R_w * I_w)

3. Asymmetric Vote-Transfer Matrix ($\alpha_p$)

Absorbs available minor protest shares (Confelicity/Heritage) first, then draws remaining support asymmetrically from rival parties ($\sum \alpha_p = 1$):

V'_{p,w} = max(0, V_{p,w} - alpha_p * max(0, V_RB - V_abs))

4. First-Past-The-Post Logistic Win Probability ($P_{\text{win}}$)

Converts the plurality margin ($M_w$) over the strongest local challenger into win probability via a sigmoid curve ($k = 0.35$):

M_w = V_RB - max(V'_rivals)   |   P_win = 100 / (1 + exp(-0.35 * M_w))
